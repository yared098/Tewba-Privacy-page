Tewba Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 26, 2026

Welcome to Tewba, an online book marketplace that allows users to discover, buy, and sell books. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use our mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Tewba, we may collect:

  • Personal information, such as your name, email address, phone number, and delivery address.
  • Account information needed to authenticate and manage your account.
  • Book listings, descriptions, prices, orders, and purchase or sales history.
  • Payment status and transaction records.
  • Technical information and app diagnostics, where applicable.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Display books and support buying and selling.
  • Process orders and coordinate deliveries.
  • Verify payments and maintain transaction records.
  • Send order updates and service notifications.
  • Provide customer support and improve our services.
  • Protect users and prevent fraud or unauthorized access.

3. Payments

Tewba may support electronic payments or other payment methods. Third-party payment providers may process payment information under their own privacy policies. We do not intentionally store full payment card details unless our actual payment arrangements expressly provide for this.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information when necessary with hosting providers, payment processors, delivery services, or legal authorities where required by law. Limited information may also be shared between buyers and sellers to complete transactions.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. However, no online system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate our services, complete transactions, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. When information is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to delete or anonymize it, subject to applicable requirements.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate information, or deletion of your account and associated information. Some transaction records may need to be retained for legal or legitimate business purposes.

8. Children's Privacy

Tewba does not knowingly collect children's personal information in violation of applicable law. If you believe that information has been collected improperly, please contact us so we can review the matter.

9. Third-Party Services

Tewba may use third-party services for hosting, authentication, notifications, analytics, payments, or delivery. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our services or legal requirements change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
